Getting good results

  • Lots of light. High-speed capture needs bright conditions — shoot in daylight or add lighting.
  • Side-on, on a tripod. Frame the ball and the bottom of the swing; keep the phone steady.
  • Use a marked/colored ball for the most reliable tracking.

The app didn't detect my impact.

Loud environments can mask the strike. Use the Manual trigger, or adjust the impact-sensitivity slider, and try again.

Where are my recordings?

Clips are saved on your device and available through the in-app share sheet and the Files app. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Which devices are supported?

A recent iPhone capable of 240 FPS video. The app does not run on the iOS Simulator.
